    How To

    1 Where you can play in Oregon

    🏈 DraftKings Sportsbook: the only legal betting appOregon lets just one company take bets online, through the Oregon Lottery. You must be 21+ and physically in Oregon. No college games in the app.
    🎰 Tribal casino sportsbooks: in personCasinos like The Mill Casino in North Bend take bets at the counter. College games are allowed there.
    📱 Pick'em apps: PrizePicks, Underdog, SleeperYou pick whether players go over or under a stat line. They run in Oregon, but state law doesn't clearly say yes or no to them, so read each app's rules before you deposit. DraftKings' fantasy games aren't offered in Oregon.
    ⚠️ Any other betting site that takes Oregon players (offshore sites, "crypto books") is illegal and might not pay you out.

    2 Your first bet on DraftKings

    1. Download DraftKings Sportsbook from the App Store.
    2. Sign up with your real name, address and the last 4 digits of your SSN. They check your ID by law.
    3. Turn on Location Services. The app has to confirm you're in Oregon.
    4. Deposit a small amount you're OK losing, like $20.
    5. Tap the odds you want. That adds it to your bet slip.
    6. Type your stake, check what you'd win, then tap Place Bet.
    7. Log it here right away (📝 on Home).

    3 How to read odds

    • Minus (-150) is the favorite. You bet $150 to win $100.
    • Plus (+130) is the underdog. A $100 bet wins $130.
    • If you win, you get your stake back plus the winnings.
    Enter odds like -110 or +145.

    4 Kinds of bets

    • Moneyline: just pick who wins.
    • Spread (Chiefs -3.5): the favorite has to win by more than 3.5. The underdog (+3.5) can lose by 3 and you still win.
    • Total / Over-Under (O 44.5): both teams' points added together, over or under the number.
    • Player props: bets on one player, like "Kupp over 5.5 catches".
    • Parlay: several bets combined, and every leg has to hit. Big payouts, but it's where the books make the most money.

    5 How pick'em apps work

    • Pick 2 to 6 players and choose More or Less on each stat line.
    • On a standard entry every pick has to hit. More picks pay more but hit far less often.
    • Use your Start/Sit projections. If a player projects well above his line, "More" might be worth a look.

    6 Rules that keep your money alive

    • Set a weekly limit (⚙️ settings). Home shows how close you are.
    • Bet about 1 to 2% of your bankroll per bet. The Bet Finder sizes it for you.
    • Never chase losses with bigger bets.
    • Track every bet. Your ROI tells you whether you're really winning.
    • Log the closing odds. A positive CLV over 50+ bets is the strongest sign you have a real edge.
    • If it stops being fun, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER, free and confidential.
